# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Readers use Readlay on phones and desktop computers to read books, listen to prepared narration and save vocabulary. The project owner maintains the catalogue and checks storage usage. The admin area serves that maintenance work.

## Product Purpose

Keep a reader's place when changing between reading and listening or between devices. The library is the home page. Authentication, progress, bookmarks and the Word Bank are working parts of the application. New features must preserve them.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, clear and focused. Use UK English and direct, literal wording. These choices come from AGENTS.md, DESIGN.md and the existing reader, rather than a new brand direction.

## Anti-references

The existing design excludes marketing feature grids, invented reading statistics, decorative waveforms and movement that distracts from reading. Admin numbers must describe measured usage or explicitly labelled reservations. Do not imply a billing guarantee.

## Design Principles

- Carry the reader's place between views without changing it during unrelated interactions.
- Reuse the existing navigation, typography and controls.
- Show actual content and clear availability states.
- Make the main task easy on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Distinguish verified information, conservative estimates and unavailable data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain the existing keyboard navigation, visible focus, text labels and reduced-motion support. Keep warnings understandable without colour. Admin controls need associated labels, clear error messages and readable mobile layouts. Do not claim an accessibility certification from these design rules.
